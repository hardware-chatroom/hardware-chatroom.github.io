# Asset notes

`public/images/workbench.webp` was generated with the built-in image generation tool for this website, then resized and encoded as WebP. It is an illustrative hardware composition, identified as such in the homepage caption.

Generation brief:

> Premium photorealistic editorial product photograph, landscape 3:2. A compact green ESP32-like development board with metal radio shield, tiny black OLED display attached to a white solderless breadboard with orange and muted green jumper wires, and a silver tactile pushbutton. Screen shows a small pixel smile, no words or logos. Elevated three-quarter camera angle, seamless white tabletop, soft daylight and grounded shadows, authentic maker materials, generous white space. Refined, restrained product photography for a light Hardware Chatroom homepage. No captions, frames, or watermark. This is illustrative hardware photography, not an actual community project.

The SVG brand mark, circuit illustration, and social preview layout are original code-native vector designs. They are independent of the photograph and can be edited without regenerating the image.
