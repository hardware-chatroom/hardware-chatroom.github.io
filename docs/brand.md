# Hardware Chatroom brand

The mark combines a conversation bubble with three circuit contacts: discussion becomes something you can connect, test, and build. The green contact adds a small note of curiosity.

- Primary name: **Hardware Chatroom · 硬件唠嗑室**.
- Tagline: **Talk about hardware. Build something fun.**
- Chinese tagline: **聊聊硬件，动手做点好玩的。**
- Mark: `public/brand/logo.svg`, scalable and suitable for the organization avatar.
- App icons: `public/icons/`; use `icon-512.png` for a square organization avatar.
- Favicon: `public/favicon.svg` and `app/favicon.ico`.
- Ink: `#171b19`; background: `#ffffff`; pale surface: `#f5f7f4`; circuit accent: `#76d5ab`.
- Keep at least one eighth of the mark's width clear around it. Avoid distorting it or placing it on a busy image.

Website, favicon, social preview, and PWA icons share this mark. Organization avatar upload is a separate GitHub organization setting; the website deployment does not change that setting.
